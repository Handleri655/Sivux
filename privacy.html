2. Data we collect

  • Contact form: name, company, email, phone, message, optional promo/UTM data
  • Site audit: email, URL, results, and technical request data as needed
  • Client portal / domain tools: account and usage data under your agreement
  • Analytics: anonymised or pseudonymised traffic data (e.g. Vercel Insights)

3. Purposes

We process data to reply to enquiries, deliver free audits, fulfil contracts, improve the site, and meet legal obligations. Legal bases include contract, legitimate interest, and consent where asked.

4. Processors

We use providers such as FormSubmit/Resend (email), Vercel (hosting/analytics), and optionally OpenAI for audit text. We do not sell personal data.

5. Cookies & tracking

We use cookies and similar storage. You can accept or decline analytics in the cookie banner, and change the choice anytime via “Cookie settings” in the footer.

  • Essential: consent choice, form helpers, campaign tags (ref/UTM) for up to 60 days
  • Analytics (consent): Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights

We do not use ad pixels by default.

6. Retention

Contact and lead data is kept only as long as needed for sales follow-up or the customer relationship, typically up to 24 months unless law requires longer.

7. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection, and lodge a complaint with the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi). Contact: info@sivux.fi.
