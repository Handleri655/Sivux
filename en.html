FAQ

Common questions before kickoff.

How much do business websites cost?

Projects typically start from around 449 EUR + VAT. Final pricing depends on page count, content scope, and integrations.

How long does a website project take?

Usually 1-3 weeks when content is ready. We aim to launch within 10 business days after kickoff when materials are delivered on schedule.

Do you build websites for small businesses?

Yes. We build websites for small businesses and growing companies, from one-page landings to multi-page service sites.

Do you work with companies across Finland?

Yes. We serve companies remotely across Finland, including Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa, Tampere, Turku, and Oulu.
